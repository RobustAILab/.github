## 🔬 About Us

The **Robust AI Lab** at **Old Dominion University**, directed by **Dr. Lusi Li**, conducts research on **robust, adaptive, and trustworthy artificial intelligence (AI)**.

Our research focuses on developing robust and trustworthy machine learning methods that enable intelligent systems to reliably **acquire, integrate, transfer, and communicate knowledge** across heterogeneous data sources, modalities, domains, and distributed environments.

We aim to advance the reliability, adaptability, and security of AI systems through fundamental machine learning research and applications in multimodal intelligence, semantic communications, and cybersecurity.

## 🎯 Research Areas

Our research interests include:

- **Multi-View and Multi-Modal Learning:** Representation learning, alignment, and fusion across heterogeneous views and modalities.
- **Transferable and Adaptive Learning:** Domain adaptation, few-shot/zero-shot learning, and adaptive knowledge transfer.
- **Trustworthy and Secure AI:** Adversarial robustness, AI security, and resilient learning systems.
- **Wireless and Semantic Communications:** Dynamic spectrum access, cross-modal semantic communications, and adversarial and backdoor attacks on AI-native semantic communication systems.
